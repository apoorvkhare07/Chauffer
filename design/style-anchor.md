# Style anchor

Medium: painterly collage
Palette: porcelain background #F7F8FA, ink #242438, cobalt accent #4149CF, periwinkle #CBD2F3, peacock green #176557
Light: soft late-afternoon light from the upper left
Materials: matte painted canvas, embroidered cotton, classical marble, hand-painted botanical motifs
Mood: playful, cultivated, personal
Never: neon, glossy 3D blobs, gradient mesh backgrounds, lens flare, floating particles, generic robots, logos or baked-in text

Subject world: Indian fashion and personal shopping, translated through classical portraits and whimsical painterly still lifes. Portrait card compositions, clear silhouettes, richly colored clothing. Imagery is brand illustration, never a representation of sample catalogue products.
